SATURDAY MOBILITY
Active Recovery~20 min
🌿

MOBILITY DAY

Light movement and stretching. The goal is to increase range of motion — directly improving your squat depth, shoulder flexibility, and hip mobility for advanced skills.

ROUTINE — TAP EACH TO CHECK OFF
Hip Flexor Lunge Stretch — kneel with one foot forward, drive hip forward and down. 2 min each side.
Deep Squat Hold — squat as low as possible, heels flat, elbows push knees out. 2–3 min total.
Pike Stretch — sit with legs straight, reach for toes. Crucial for L-sit. 2 min.
Wrist Circles & Stretches — rotate, flex, extend. Your wrists carry all handstand weight. 2 min.
Shoulder Dislocates (towel) — hold towel wide, rotate overhead and behind. 10 reps.
Cat-Cow Spine Waves — on all fours, arch and round. 10 reps, slow.
Thoracic Rotation — sit cross-legged, hands behind head, rotate upper back. 8 reps each.

FULL REST DAY

Rest is when your muscles grow and your nervous system consolidates movement patterns. Walk, sleep, eat well. Come back Monday ready.

🧠
Mental Rehearsal
Optional · 5–10 min

Visualise performing each exercise with perfect form. Research shows mental rehearsal genuinely improves physical skill. Pick one movement — handstand, pistol squat, L-sit — and run through 5–10 perfect reps in your mind in detail.

Golden Rules
⚖️
Stop at 80%
Always leave 2–3 reps in the tank. Never train to failure.
📏
Form First
One clean rep beats five sloppy ones. Bad habits take months to undo.
🐢
Progress Slowly
Move up only when the current level feels genuinely easy.
😴
Sleep is Training
8 hours accelerates skill acquisition dramatically.
🔄
Consistency Wins
20 min daily for a year beats a 2-hour session once a week.
